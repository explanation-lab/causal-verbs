# Historical studies and pilots

These are source-designated older versions, pilots and supporting results. Preserve their identity and scientific content; do not treat them as deployed/current versions. The original archive READMEs remain historical documentation and may refer to former paths or excluded restricted files.

- [study1_verbal](study1_verbal/): earlier verbal chain/psychological-verb pilots and coded response derivatives.
- [study2_verbal](study2_verbal/): historical intentional/accidental and related verbal variants, response data and figures.
- [study3_nonverbal](study3_nonverbal/): early adult, omission, child, pre-batch-3 and explicit-rating variants.
- [single_cause/nonverbal_standalone_pilot](single_cause/nonverbal_standalone_pilot/): earlier standalone nonverbal single-cause implementation and materials.
- [adult_pilot_early_handcoded](adult_pilot_early_handcoded/): preserved early hand-coded distribution reports.

Participant identifiers and source recording filenames were sanitized; identifiable session exports, a participant lookup table and unverified generic sample media were excluded. [SOURCE_MAPPING.md](../docs/handoff/SOURCE_MAPPING.md) accounts for every file.
