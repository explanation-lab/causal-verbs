## Quantities, intervals and sample limitations

- Raw Yes=1 / No=0. Average available scenarios within participant before group means; both-Yes and neither-Yes responses are retained. Distal/proximal are repeated measures, not separate participants.
- Causal-role differentiation = [(Caused Distal − Proximal) + (Lexical Proximal − Distal)] / 2. Fault and Punish distal preferences = Distal − Proximal. Percentage ticks on contrasts denote percentage points.
- The centered payoff uses responsibility Yes (%) − 50 and causal-role differentiation (%) / 2. Zero means 50/50 responsibility responding or no causal differentiation. These are different constructs on a shared display scale, not interchangeable measurements.
- Blue/red fills in the payoff are the areas between each context's Intentional and Accidental means (alpha .18), not confidence bands. They sit behind all data marks; red overlays blue.
- All estimates and 95% participant-bootstrap limits are reused unchanged from the source workflow (5,000 draws). Developmental mean/trajectory CIs are thin vertical bars without caps or endpoint marks. The two age-adjusted regressions are the explicit exception: they retain their original translucent 95% CI ribbons and complete prediction grids; all predictions/limits remain in the exact-value CSVs. Age-adjusted fits and predictions are reused directly; the pooled relationship backup retains its unchanged source linear-fit drawing layer.
- Final figures and builds omit the redundant Age axis title. Legends use 44-point text with larger keys, except the two original-style age-adjusted regressions, which retain their original sizing. Informative non-age x-axis labels are retained. The centered synthesis wraps its five-entry legend into two rows; raw figures retain space for participant counts.
- The matched main figure uses a 28 × 11 inch canvas, 40-point tick labels and 44-point axis/panel labels. The two age-adjusted figures and their builds instead match the original 20 × 11 inch PDFs: white panels, 2.1 mm fitted lines, original faint participant points, translucent CI ribbons, axis limits, fonts, spacing and Study 2 line-only legend. Only the top explanatory sentence and footer are removed; their layout space is retained so all other elements stay in the original positions.
- Age-adjusted figures are children-only: Study 1 responsibility ~ differentiation + numeric age; Study 2 responsibility ~ differentiation × intention + numeric age. Faint points are observed participant scores with unchanged display-only jitter; fitted lines use the original age standardization. Unchanged coefficients, contrasts and joint frequencies are supplied in the root CSVs. These exploratory cross-sectional associations do not establish causation or mediation.
- Single Cause child data are pilot data and cover ages 3–6 only. Binned comparisons use participant-weighted 3–4 and 5–6 groups, excluding ages 7–9. Single-year figures show adults separately; binned figures retain their source categorical connections.
- Study 2 adult chain values use the selected available adult pilot; the main adult dataset remains missing. Single Cause uses all 32 available adult records; the historical N=30 exclusion subset remains unidentified. No historical exclusions or preregistration decisions are invented.
- The organized import is complete; Sunny's scientific handoff gaps remain unresolved. This presentation pass does not imply scientific handoff acceptance. Existing missing scripts, custody, path, timing, asset-source/hosting and preregistration/cohort limitations remain as documented elsewhere.
