## What the shared scale means

The four colored trajectories are **raw Yes-response percentages**, calculated
by averaging scenarios within participant, then participants within bin and
condition. They are not Intentional-minus-Accidental differences.

The gray trajectory is the **Study 1 Baseline** composite:

```text
((Caused Distal - Caused Proximal) + (Lexical Proximal - Lexical Distal)) / 2
```

Compute this within each participant, then average participant scores within bin.
Multiplying by 100 expresses the composite in percentage points. Its means are
13.89% (3–4), 41.95% (5–6) and 33.50% (Adult), with baseline Ns of 63, 59 and 100.
The same mean, N and interval are repeated in both panels solely as a visual
reference; duplication does not create additional observations.

The quantities share a numeric axis while retaining their different meanings:
the gray score is a causal-role contrast, not a Yes-response rate. The axis title
and caption identify both quantities. The baseline is not subtracted from any
responsibility rate, and equal heights do not imply identical constructs.

## Uncertainty and provenance

All 95% participant-bootstrap CIs are copied unchanged from V1, including all
24 responsibility endpoints and the three baseline endpoints (each displayed
twice here). These are pointwise intervals for each mean, not tests of gaps
between curves. The 50% horizontal line is a visual guide, not a chance-model
assumption. No interval is clipped.

Participants receive equal weights within each child bin, rather than equal
weighting of single-year means. Ages 7–9 do not enter this figure. The figure
explicitly notes that **Single Cause child data are pilot data**. Chain Adult
values still come from the selected Study 2 adult pilot; the main adult dataset
remains missing. Single Cause adults still include all 32 recovered records,
with the historical N=30 subset unresolved. All other sampling, privacy and
scientific handoff conclusions remain unchanged; see [V1 methods](README.md).

## Reproduction and validation

Run the [existing R Markdown workflow](../../../code/R/README.md) from the
repository root:

```sh
Rscript --vanilla -e 'rmarkdown::render("current/code/R/causal_verbs.Rmd")'
```

Embedded checks require exact V1 identity of every mean, N and interval; an
identical baseline reference in both panels; two panels with five trajectories
and three points per trajectory; identical axes; 50% reference lines; and every
CI bound within 0–100%. Independent CSV comparison checks all 30 plotted rows
against the saved V1 table. Existing figure/output and source-data hashes are
verified before committing. Both final PDF and PNG are visually reviewed.
