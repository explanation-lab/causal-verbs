Average available scenarios within participant × measure × role first, then average participant means within each plotted cell. If one scenario is missing, use the available response; if both are missing, omit only that measure/role mean. The current plotted samples have both scenarios and identical eligible/contributing Ns. Each point has a 5,000-replicate participant-bootstrap 95% percentile interval. Endpoints shared across new figures reuse the same summary and CI. Intervals are pointwise mean intervals, not paired-difference intervals or tests.

Punishment compares Single Cause, Chain: Distal and Chain: Proximal. Intentional/Accidental varies the judged person's own intention for the first two; **for Proximal it varies the distal agent's intention**. The horizontal Single Cause figure compares intention conditions separately within each measure. The role-mapping figure pairs Distal and Proximal within each Baseline/Intentional/Accidental position; it does not connect conditions. Group-mean connectors do not imply within-participant condition changes. All figures retain light uncertainty intervals; visual inspection found them readable without separate no-CI versions.

Punishment separates more strongly between intention conditions at ages 5–6 than 3–4 for Single Cause and Distal, while Proximal follows a different pattern. Single Cause lexical endorsement is high in both conditions, with a smaller gap at 5–6 and identical adult means; Fault/Punish show larger older-child/adult gaps. In the chain figure, Caused favors Distal throughout the displayed cells, but Lexical's Proximal preference is not universal: Intentional ages 3–4 are tied, and Intentional adults slightly favor Distal. These are descriptive cross-sectional patterns from different cohorts, not demonstrated developmental or between-study causal effects.

PNG resolutions: 3840 × 2160 for punishment and Single Cause; 3840 × 2520 for role mapping. PDFs are vector graphics.
