Colors distinguish series in this new figure; Single Cause is blue here because gray is reserved for the reference. Earlier color mappings are unchanged.

All quantities are percentage-point contrasts, with percentage tick labels on a common **-25% to 100%** y axis. The large black-outlined points and thick lines follow the existing talk style. Childhood shading ends after age 6, before Adult. Adults are separate reference points without child-to-adult connecting lines. The figure has one centered legend and a strong black dashed 0% line in each panel, with no title, subtitle or footer.

## Participant-first computation

The underlying binary answers remain Yes = 1 and No = 0; neither responses remain included. Existing participant means first average available scenarios within participant and judgment (and role for Study 2). Intention effects then subtract the means of the separate Intentional and Accidental participant groups within each age and context. The manipulation is between participants, so this is not a within-person condition difference.

The main talk plot intentionally omits intervals. For the table, 5,000 bootstrap draws independently resample participants within each age × condition × context, preserving each participant's Fault and Punish scores together. Each draw subtracts condition means; its 2.5th/97.5th percentiles provide the 95% interval. This includes uncertainty in both groups, rather than subtracting separate CI endpoints. Seed: `20260920 + 24`. The existing baseline intervals are reused unchanged. Intervals are pointwise descriptive uncertainty; household dependence is not modeled.

## Samples and interpretation

- **Study 1 reference:** child Ns 31, 32, 29 and 30 at ages 3–6, plus 100 adults.
- **Single Cause:** 96 children aged 3–6 and all 32 recovered adults (16 per condition). The historical adult N=30 subset remains unidentified.
- **Chain: Distal:** 223 children aged 3–6 (107 Intentional, 116 Accidental), plus the same 41-person Study 2 adult pilot (20 Intentional, 21 Accidental). The preregistered main adult dataset remains missing.

Exact age/condition Ns are in the CSV. Eligibility rules and the existing uncapped descriptive snapshot are unchanged. The reference and intention effects are different quantities, shown in common units; their visual alignment does not test a within-participant association or establish that differentiation causes intention sensitivity. All prior scientific handoff gaps remain unresolved.


The workflow checks all 20 intention effects and their Ns against the independently validated raw Yes summaries, checks exact reference identity between panels, and verifies 30 points, 24 child line vertices, and common axis geometry. New tables contain aggregate values only, with no participant identifiers.
